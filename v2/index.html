MISSION BRIEFING

In this simulation you'll step into the role of the new Business Unit Director for JCB's European Division.

Your predecessor delivered steady growth. The Exec has set an ambitious growth target for the year ahead — and expects you to hit it without burning out the team along the way.

The Exec's Expectations: Deliver the growth plan and leave the division in good shape. Every decision you make will show up in the business — in growth, margin, morale, attrition and capability. The Exec will look at the results you deliver and the state you leave the business in.

WHAT THE EXEC WANTS: Hit the growth target and keep the team healthy and motivated while you do it. Push too hard and the business will feel it; coast and the numbers won't land.

The pressure is real: the market is competitive, the team is watching, and the Exec is expecting results.

You have full authority over the division:

  • Portfolio: Loadalls, Backhoe Loaders, Excavators, Loading Shovels
  • Team: 87 staff across sales, operations, purchasing, engineering and service
  • Network: 250 dealers across 12 European markets

Your Mission: Lead the division through the challenges ahead and show what the JCB leadership framework looks like in action.
