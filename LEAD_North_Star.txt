# JCB LEAD Framework - North Star Document
Leaders at JCB show up by demonstrating the LEAD framework:

## Leadership
Our people will show potential to lead both internal and external customers and stakeholders to achieve business objectives. They will show up as a leader if they:
- **Are courageous** and consistently motivate, inspire and influence their team and the people around them to achieve both their personal and organisational needs
- **Show vulnerability**
- **Build positive inclusive relationships** - Both professional and personal relationships within and outside the organisation should be built to support individual and organisational success
- **Adapt their style** to that of others to build rapport and ability to influence
- **Have commercial and technical acumen** - The ability to make sound commercial and technical decisions, and operates as if it is your own company
- **Consider the bigger picture** and the implications their decisions will have on business, people and customers
- **Are ethical and sustainable** - Leads in an ethical, inclusive and sustainable way, giving praise and addressing poor performance
- **Do things right, or do the right thing** according to the situation

## Excellence
Displaying emotional intelligence and being able to use this to successfully handle challenges and make effective decisions. This encompasses:
- **Self-awareness** - Having conscious knowledge of your own strengths and development areas and being able to use these insights to manage situations appropriately
- **Aware of the shadow they cast** as a leader
- **Demonstrates critical thinking** - Ability to think long term, taking a broader perspective whilst setting priorities for short term and long term strategic goals
- **Informed decision making** - Able to analyse, remove and solve problems, making critical data driven decisions, providing conclusions based on evidence and logic
- **Courageous** - Not afraid to speak up and listens openly and is willing to learn from mistakes
- **Owns decisions** and holds self and other people to account
- **"Creates allies"** by standing by others, even when not present
- **"Remains positive"** when delivering unpopular messages even they may not agree with them

## Agility
Moving quickly and easily and being effective in handling ambiguity and never giving up:
- **Navigating ambiguity** - Their ability to handle unclear or unpredictable situations without always having the necessary information to hand
- **Considers how VUCA** (volatility, uncertainty, complexity, and ambiguity) could affect the organisation
- **Adaptability** - Their ability to be pragmatic and drive change and is dynamic in constantly evolving situations
- **Generates new and creative solutions** to problems
- **Resilience** - Their ability to recover quickly from set-backs and to be able to cope with high pressure demands. Able to persevere in the face of adversity and recognise where others may need support
- **Takes initiative** - Is proactive to take the initiative and seeks responsibility without being asked to do so. Encourages proactivity in others

## Determination
Having relentless determination to learn, grow and success:
- **"Drive to excel"** - Strong will to achieve excellence in whatever they set out to do
- **"Always passionate"** - Supports others to achieve more
- **Communicates openly and often**
- **Has a growth mind-set**
- **Fosters learning and growth** - Continually seeks opportunities to learn. Actively seeks feedback from others to improve own performance
- **Innovates** - Challenges the way it has always been done and looks for a better way
- **Accepts personal failure** may come from trying to innovate, but sees failure as a learning tool for business growth and personal development
- **Is a thinking performer** - has a positive outlook and is able to deal with challenges with self-belief and drive to achieve goals

**Note**: Each of these definitions (Leadership, Excellence, Agility, Determination) should form the framework for the feedback provided throughout the JCB Leadership Challenge game.
